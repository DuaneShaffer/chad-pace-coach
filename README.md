# Chad Pace Coach

A phone app for the CrossFit Hero WOD **Chad** (1,000 step-ups): the camera counts your reps, a pacing engine tracks you against a target time, and a voice coach keeps you on pace.

**Try it:** https://duaneshaffer.github.io/chad-pace-coach/ — on iPhone, open in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen.
